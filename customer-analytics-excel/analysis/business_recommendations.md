# Business Recommendations

## 1. Strengthen the highest-performing lead sources

Use Inbound Calls and Referrals as internal benchmarks because both exceed the 6.6% overall conversion rate. Review their lead quality, sales handling, and follow-up process before scaling other channels.

## 2. Focus sales enablement on stronger products

Cybersecurity Services, Business Consulting, and CRM Software all convert at roughly 7.2% or higher. Review successful pitches, customer objections, and follow-up patterns for these products and apply useful practices to weaker categories.

## 3. Prioritize the pending pipeline

There are **2,742 leads marked Pending**. Create a follow-up queue and prioritize leads using product, lead source, customer type, region, and available call information.

## 4. Replicate Southwest practices

Southwest combines the highest regional revenue (**$6.27M**) with an above-average conversion rate (**7.0%**). Compare its sales practices with the Southeast, which converts at **6.1%**, to identify actionable differences.

## 5. Improve existing-customer conversion

Existing Customers convert at **6.1%**, below New Customers at **7.3%**. Review retention and upsell processes and determine whether existing customers are being contacted with offers that match their needs.
