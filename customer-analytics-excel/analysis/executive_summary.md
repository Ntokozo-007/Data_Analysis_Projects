# Executive Summary

## Bottom Line

The business generated **$29.46M from 10,936 leads**, but only **726 leads converted, producing a 6.6% overall conversion rate**. The strongest opportunity is to improve the conversion of the existing pipeline: **2,742 leads are currently marked as Pending follow-up**, while Inbound Calls, Cybersecurity Services, and the Southwest region are already showing above-average performance that can be used as benchmarks.

## Key Findings

### 1. Conversion is the primary constraint
Only **6.6% of 10,936 leads converted**. Because most leads did not convert, even a modest improvement in the conversion process could materially increase the number of customers without requiring the same increase in lead generation.

### 2. Inbound Calls outperform the overall benchmark
Inbound Calls generated **102 conversions from 1,361 leads**, giving a **7.5% conversion rate**, compared with 6.6% overall. The channel also generated **$4.30M**, making it the strongest lead source by revenue.

### 3. Cybersecurity Services has the highest product conversion
Cybersecurity Services converted **7.7% of 1,343 leads**, compared with **5.6% for Digital Marketing Services**, the lowest-performing product by conversion rate. The business should examine the sales process and customer proposition behind the stronger products.

### 4. Southwest leads regional revenue performance
Southwest generated **$6.27M from 2,187 leads** and converted **7.0%** of them. Its performance is above the overall 6.6% benchmark and makes it a useful region for identifying practices that may work elsewhere.

### 5. Existing customers have weaker conversion
New Customers converted at **7.3%**, while Existing Customers converted at **6.1%**. This gap suggests that the existing-customer journey should be reviewed for retention and upsell opportunities.

## Recommended Actions

- Prioritize Inbound Calls and Referrals for sales follow-up and channel review.
- Review the sales process used for Cybersecurity Services, Business Consulting, and CRM Software.
- Work through the **2,742 pending follow-ups** using a defined priority process.
- Benchmark lower-performing regions against Southwest.
- Launch a targeted existing-customer review focused on retention and upselling.
