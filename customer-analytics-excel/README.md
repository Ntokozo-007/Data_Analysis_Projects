## Executive Overview

This project analyzes **10,936 customer leads** using Microsoft Excel to understand lead conversion, revenue performance, customer types, products/services, regions, and acquisition sources.

The central business question is:

> **Where is the business generating the strongest revenue and conversion performance, and where should management focus sales and follow-up efforts?**

The analysis found that the business generated **$29.46M in revenue**, but only **726 of 10,936 leads converted (6.6%)**. This points to a substantial opportunity to improve the performance of the existing lead pipeline rather than relying only on additional lead generation.

## Executive Findings

### 1. Conversion is the main growth opportunity

Only **6.6% of 10,936 leads converted**, leaving more than 10,000 leads without a recorded conversion. Improving follow-up, sales handling, and qualification could increase revenue without requiring the same increase in lead volume.

### 2. Inbound calls are the strongest acquisition source

Inbound Calls produced **102 conversions from 1,361 leads**, a **7.5% conversion rate**, and generated **$4.30M**. Outbound Campaigns and Referrals also performed above the overall conversion rate, making these channels strong candidates for greater attention.

### 3. Cybersecurity Services leads product conversion

Cybersecurity Services converted **7.7% of 1,343 leads**, the highest rate among the products/services analyzed. Business Consulting and CRM Software also converted above 7%, suggesting that sales teams should examine what makes these offers more successful.

### 4. Southwest is the strongest revenue region

The Southwest generated **$6.27M from 2,187 leads** and achieved a **7.0% conversion rate**, above the overall 6.6% rate. This makes the region a useful benchmark for understanding practices that could potentially be replicated elsewhere.

### 5. New customers convert better than existing customers

New Customers converted at **7.3%**, compared with **6.1% for Existing Customers**. Existing-customer performance therefore deserves a targeted review, particularly around retention, upselling, and follow-up activity.

## Recommendations

1. **Prioritize Inbound Calls and Referrals** when allocating sales attention because both have conversion rates above the 6.6% overall benchmark.
2. **Review the sales approach for Cybersecurity Services, Business Consulting, and CRM Software** and identify practices that can be replicated across weaker products.
3. **Investigate the 2,742 pending follow-ups** and create a structured follow-up priority list based on lead source, product, region, and customer type.
4. **Use Southwest as a performance benchmark** and compare its sales process with lower-performing regions.
5. **Create a targeted existing-customer campaign** to address the 6.1% conversion rate and identify upsell or retention opportunities.
